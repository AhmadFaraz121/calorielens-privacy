Calorie Lens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Who we are

Calorie Lens (“the app”) is provided by Ahmed Faraz, based in Pakistan. For any privacy question, contact kamranahmad709@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Without an account, your meal history, weight and water logs stay on your phone.
  • If you create an account, they are saved to our servers so they survive a lost phone and appear when you sign in on a new one. Your meal photos never leave your phone.
  • Food photos are sent for analysis and are not stored on our servers.
  • We create an anonymous account so we can count your daily scans. No name or email is needed unless you choose to add one.
  • One device at a time can be signed in to an account.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, which uses advertising identifiers.
  • If the app crashes we send a diagnostic report, with personal details stripped out.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What the app stores on your device only

The following never leaves your phone, whether or not you have an account. It is held in the app’s local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Photos you attach to logged meals. These are never uploaded. A meal viewed on a different device shows without its picture.
  • Your theme preference and your daily streak

We have no copy of either and cannot recover them for you.

What is saved to your account

If you use Calorie Lens as a guest, everything below also stays on your phone only, and uninstalling deletes it.

If you create an account, we store the following so it survives a lost phone and appears when you sign in on a new one:

  • Your meal log — food names, calories, macronutrients, ingredients, meal types and times
  • Body weight entries and water intake
  • Your profile — sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal
  • Your calorie and macronutrient targets, and your daily reminder time

Access is restricted to your own account at the database level. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to target advertising.

What is sent to our servers

Food photos and text descriptions. When you scan a meal, the image or the text you typed is sent to our analysis service, which forwards it to Google’s Gemini AI to estimate nutrition. The image is processed in memory and returned as nutrition figures. We do not store your photos, and they are not used to train any model by us. Google’s handling of the data it receives is governed by the Google APIs Terms of Service and their Privacy Policy.

An anonymous account identifier. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account with our backend provider, Supabase. This is a random identifier — it contains no personal information and is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device.

A daily scan count. We record how many analyses you have run today, against that identifier, in order to enforce the free daily limit and to know whether you have an active subscription. Nothing about what you scanned is recorded — only how many times.

Your email address, only if you choose to add one. If you create an account to keep your history and subscription across devices, we store the email address and a securely hashed password. We do not send marketing email. The only email we ever send is a password reset link, and only when you ask for one.

An installation identifier. A random string generated on your device, used to enforce one signed-in device per account — see Devices below. It is not a hardware identifier, not your advertising ID, and cannot identify you outside Calorie Lens.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, an independent open food database, to look up the product. No account identifier or personal information is sent with it. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, Google may access and use an advertising identifier on your device, and may collect information about your interaction with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a US state with applicable privacy law, the app shows you a consent form the first time it runs, and you can change your answer at any time from Profile → Ad privacy → Manage ad privacy choices. Where you have not consented to personalised advertising, we request non-personalised ads only.

Google’s use of advertising data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Premium subscribers see no ads.

Crash reporting

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, we send a diagnostic report to Sentry so we can fix it. A report contains the error, where in the code it happened, your device model and OS version, and your anonymous account identifier.

Before anything is sent, it is filtered to strip email addresses, file paths, access tokens, API keys and image data. Food photos are never included in a crash report.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google — we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to verify your purchase receipt and to record whether your subscription is active, stored against your anonymous account identifier.

Health information

Without an account, your meal log, body weight and water intake stay on your device. We do not receive them, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

With an account, they are stored on our servers, as listed under What is saved to your account. This is health-related information and we treat it accordingly: it is restricted to your own account at the database level, it is never shared with any third party, and it is never used for advertising.

Separately, and whether or not you have an account: when you run a scan, the photo you take or the description you type — “two eggs and toast” — is sent for analysis exactly as described under What is sent to our servers. It produces one set of nutrition figures and is not stored.

Your meal photos are never uploaded, with or without an account.

Calorie Lens is not a medical device. Nutrition values are AI estimates, not measurements, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet, particularly if you have a health condition.

Devices

Calorie Lens allows one signed-in device per account at a time. When you sign in on a new device, any other device signed in to that account is signed out, and that account’s meal history, weight, profile and reminder are removed from it.

To do this we store a random identifier for your installation of the app, generated on your device. It is not a hardware identifier, it is not your advertising ID, and it cannot be used to identify you outside Calorie Lens. It is deleted when you delete your account or uninstall the app.

How long we keep things

  • On-device data, including meal photos: until you delete it in the app or uninstall
  • Meal log, weight, water, profile and reminder, if you have an account: until you delete the entry, or delete your account
  • Scan counts: retained against your anonymous identifier while your account exists
  • Account email, if you added one: until you ask us to delete it
  • Food photos sent for analysis: not retained

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to processing.

You can exercise most of these yourself: History → Clear all deletes your meal log, on this device and from your account. Premium subscribers can export the full log as CSV from Trends → Export. For deletion, see Deleting your data and your account below.

Deleting your data and your account

You can erase most of it yourself at any time:

  • Your meal log — History → Clear all. This deletes it from your account too, not just from this device.
  • Everything stored locally, including your meal photos — uninstall the app. Note that if you have an account, uninstalling does not delete what is on our servers; use Delete account below for that.

To delete everything at once, including the account itself:

  • In the app — Profile → Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your meal history, weight, water, profile, reminder, account identifier, daily scan count, subscription status and email address, along with everything stored locally. It cannot be undone.
  • By email — if you can no longer open the app, write to kamranahmad709@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Calorie Lens account” and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

You do not need an account to make this request, and you do not have to give a reason. Deleting your server-side data does not cancel a Google Play subscription — manage that in the Play Store under Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.
